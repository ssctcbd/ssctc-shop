Privacy policy

This page covers the shop management system of SS Computer Training Centre, Baunia, Kalibari, Dhaka, at shop.sscomputertrainingcentre.com, and its "SSCTC Shop Backup" connection to Google Drive.

What the system keeps

The shop's own business records: sales, payments, dues, stock, expenses, staff accounts, and the names and mobile numbers of customers who buy from the shop. Customers can see their own bills in the customer portal.

Google Drive

The shop owner connects the system to the shop's own Google account so that a copy of the database is saved there every night, in a folder called "SSCTC Shop backups".

  • The only access requested is drive.file: the system can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot read anything else in that Google Drive.
  • No Google user data is shared with anyone, sold, or used for advertising or for any purpose other than this backup.
  • The access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

The shop does not sell or give these records to anyone. They are kept on the shop's hosting account and in its backups.

Contact

For any question, or to have your details corrected, speak to the shop: SS Computer Training Centre, Baunia, Kalibari, Dhaka.
