5. Payment ar baki r niyom

Transaction ID dilei payment "hoye geche" dhora hobe na

Cash hate pele shathe shathe verified. bKash, Nagad, Bank ar Other age unverified thakbe, jotokkhon na keu taka asheche dekhe verify kore.

Default niyom: shudhu verified payment e baki kombe. Unverified taka alada kore "verify er opekkhay" dekhabe.

Ekta payment koyekta purono bill e bhag hote pare

Jemon: customer 1,000 taka dilo. 600 diye ekta purono bill shesh, baki 400 arekta bill e gelo. System shobcheye purono bill age dhorbe, tumi chaile bodlate parbe.

Kono bikri ba payment mocha jabe na — bhul hole karon likhe "void" korte hobe, itihash theke jabe.
